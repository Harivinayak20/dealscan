# Dealscan.dev Directory & Tool-Listing Outreach

Goal: backlinks + referral traffic for dealscan.dev (free used-car deal analyzer and price checker, no signup).

Status legend: **submitted** (done via curl), **needs-account** (requires signup/login, manual), **manual-form** (public form, no account, but not curl-able — JS/captcha/review queue), **skipped-paid** (paid-only tier for a real listing).

No submissions were made via curl. Every directory below either requires an account or renders its submit form via JavaScript (React/Next app), which cannot be reliably POSTed with curl without risking a malformed/spam-flagged entry. All are marked for manual submission by the owner. None required creating an account on my end (none were created).

## Listing Kit (reusable copy — use as-is across submissions)

**URL:** https://dealscan.dev

**Name/tagline variants:**
1. DealScan — Check any used car deal before you sign
2. DealScan — Free used-car price and fee checker
3. DealScan — Know if a used-car listing is a fair price

**Category suggestions:** Automotive / Car Buying, Finance & Calculators, Consumer Tools, Free Tools

**50-word description:**
DealScan is a free tool that analyzes used-car listings to tell you if the price is fair. Paste a listing and DealScan checks it against market data and breaks down dealer fees, so you know what you're actually paying for before you negotiate or sign. No signup required.

**150-word description:**
Buying a used car usually means guessing whether the sticker price is fair and untangling a stack of dealer fees that aren't always explained. DealScan is a free web tool that removes the guesswork. Paste in a used-car listing and DealScan analyzes the price against comparable market data to flag whether it's a good deal, overpriced, or in line with expectations. It also breaks down common dealer fees, so buyers can see what each line item actually covers and where there's room to push back. There's no account, no signup, and no cost. It's built for anyone about to buy a used car who wants a fast, honest second opinion before they negotiate or sign paperwork. DealScan doesn't sell cars, doesn't take dealer referral fees for its analysis, and doesn't claim to do anything beyond price-checking and fee transparency — just a straightforward tool to help buyers walk into a deal informed.

1. Product Hunt (traffic spike)
2. AlternativeTo (list as alternative to Kelley Blue Book / Carfax / CoPilot — long-tail comparison searches)
3. SaaSHub, StackShare (dofollow-eligible, decent DA)
4. Smol Launch, Launching Next, DevHunt (free dofollow, low effort)
5. Awesome-list GitHub PRs (#34, #35) — free dofollow, 10-min PRs
6. r/personalfinance and r/whatcarshouldIbuy wiki pitches — high-traffic referral even if nofollow
7. Everything else as time allows

Most of these are genuinely ~5-minute manual tasks once an account exists (18-20 of the 30 needs-account entries reuse the same basic Google/GitHub sign-in flow). The 9 manual-form/editorial entries are closer to outreach emails than form fills and should be treated as a separate light PR pass, not a directory sprint.
