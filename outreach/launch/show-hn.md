## Body draft

---
I have been building a free used-car deal checker (paste a listing, get a fair-price verdict and a plain-English breakdown of the dealer fees). While working on the fee side I could not find a clean, current dataset on how much dealer documentation fees actually vary between states, so I compiled one for all 50.

The short version:

- Doc fees range roughly 11x, from about $85 in California to as high as $999 in Florida.
- Only 16 states cap them.
- Every one of the ten highest-fee states has no cap.

The full breakdown is at dealscan.dev/research/dealer-fees-by-state, with the per-state numbers and sources.

The tool itself is free with no signup: you paste a listing and it returns a fair-price verdict plus the fee breakdown. There is also a fee glossary, some model comparison pages, and an embeddable price-checker widget.

I would genuinely like criticism on two things: whether the fair-price verdict holds up on listings you actually know, and whether the state fee figures match what you have been charged in your state. If a number is wrong or stale, tell me and point me at the source - I will fix it.
---
