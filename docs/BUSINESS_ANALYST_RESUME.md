## Strategic Assets

1. **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server** — First used-car tool with a native endpoint for AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, IDE tools). No competitor offers this. Creates a unique distribution channel through AI search (GEO).

2. **Multi-Provider AI Architecture** — Groq for speed (10× faster than OpenAI), Gemini as zero-cost failover, local heuristic as permanent zero-dependency fallback. No single point of AI failure.

3. **Programmatic SEO at Scale** — Template-driven content engine generating 500–2,500 unique pages with proper Schema.org markup. Built in weeks what would take a content team 6–12 months.

4. **Data Moat (DealScan Index)** — Each scan feeds a proprietary dataset of real listing prices, red flags, and market patterns. Quarterly public data studies become link-building engine.

5. **Warm Editorial Luxury Brand** — Deliberate departure from "tech-blue SaaS" to warm, premium print-like aesthetic (bone paper, espresso ink, cognac clay). Trust signals for a high-stakes purchase decision.
