## Pitch templates (under 120 words each)

### Journalist template
Subject: Free-to-cite dataset - real used-car listing scores ([PLACEHOLDER: stat])

Hi [FIRST NAME] - saw your recent piece on [SPECIFIC ARTICLE/TOPIC]. DealScan.dev runs an AI deal-score on real used-car listings, and we just published The DealScan Index - live, anonymized aggregates: [PLACEHOLDER: stat, e.g., "X% of listings score as good deals, average score Y/100"]. No survey data, no self-reported numbers - it's what real listings actually look like. Free to cite with attribution to DealScan.dev; happy to pull a custom cut (by state, model, or price band) if useful for your piece. Full page: https://dealscan.dev/research. Let me know if it's a fit.
